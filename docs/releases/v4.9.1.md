## Tariffs and plan changes

- Added The Free 4 Plan and Basic Free 4, with the full 11am-3pm free window.
- Existing entries detect recognised OVO plan switches automatically unless a
  plan was explicitly selected in Configure.
- Completed agreements no longer mask a newer active product when OVO retains
  more than one agreement, and automatically managed rates refresh with it.
- A Free 3/Free 4 period is always reported at $0, even if a generic API rate
  field contains an unrelated non-zero placeholder.
- Free 3 now reports configured Peak and Off-Peak periods and their correct
  rates instead of calling every paid period Standard.
- A paid One Plan or EV super-off-peak rate is no longer labelled FREE. The
  United Energy One Plan structure reported in #79 shows its paid 11am-4pm
  rate and never invents an EV period.
- Plan Information and Current Tariff Period include their applicable schedule
  windows as attributes; no standalone rate entities were added back.

OVO publishes fixed Free 3, Free 4 and EV product windows, but its API does not
return every distributor-specific peak boundary. Configure optional Peak Window
Start/End hours when your plan has a TOU peak. These hours also split historical
usage that OVO reports only as `OTHER`.

## Home Assistant fixes

- The documented and HACS-enforced minimum is now Home Assistant 2026.8.3.
  Older Container releases can fail before setup while resolving PyJWT (#81).
- The account device is created before category and vehicle child devices,
  and those children use Home Assistant's current `via_device_id` link.
- Multi-account matching never borrows balance or vehicle identity data from a
  different active account.
- v4.9's registry migration removes the 161 rotating Day 1-7/hourly-day
  entities responsible for v4.8.2 state-class repairs. After upgrading and
  restarting, Home Assistant may still offer to delete their orphaned long-term
  statistics. Those statistics belonged to moving labels and can be removed;
  current stable entities and normal state history are unaffected.

## Hourly-data resilience

- A transient hourly request failure or empty response retains the last good
  payload and marks it stale instead of replacing real readings with zeros.
- On a cold start, hourly-only entities remain unavailable until real hourly
  data arrives.
- Integration Health and privacy-safe diagnostics distinguish fresh, cached
  stale, and unavailable hourly data.
